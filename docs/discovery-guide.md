# Site Selection Discovery Guide

> **Goal:** Run discovery 101 and get answers to the highlighted questions before engaging a Geospatial SME.

---

## Q1: Does the customer have physical locations?

**No →** There's opportunity for **targeted marketing campaigns** for online only businesses using loyalty data. Separate use case — not site selection.

**Yes →** Continue to Q2.

---

## Q2: How do you select sites for expansion/consolidation today?

### a. No data-driven approach

> **Q3: Any pain points?**
> *Losses because of selecting locations that are not a great fit. Do not have visibility into areas that we would generally not consider.*

**→** Running a data driven Site Selection approach allows you to evaluate 1Million+ locations to see if it's the right fit for you. It reduces manual involvement of the top down approach (Market level analysis → real estate locations that are available) to a bottom up approach (scans all possible locations in your target market, including areas that you would generally not consider but you have the right demographic + geographic profile for success, leaving you with exact neighborhoods where you need to find real estate).

---

### b. Specialized GIS tools

> **Q3: Any pain points?**
> *Per seat licensing / moving data outside of ESRI costs more / not able to scale / not able to move beyond traditional analytics because of the data silos.*

**→** We have a lot of customers who use ESRI and have seen success in a better together approach. Whether it is bringing data from ESRI to Databricks or using the **ArcGIS GeoAnalytics Engine in Databricks**. If interested, **let's bring in a SME for a follow up conversation**.
